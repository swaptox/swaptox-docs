# Timelock Governance

**Deployment status: TBD.**

The Timelock governance model requires **2-of-3 multisig approval** for protocol administration and enforces a delay of at least **3 days**.

## Governance rules

- Administrative operations are limited to whitelisted contracts and functions.
- Operations require 2-of-3 multisig approval.
- Each operation must wait at least 3 days before execution.
- An emergency Guardian mechanism supports ownership recovery if multisig governance becomes unavailable.
